V'S STORY

V is the protagonist of Cyberpunk 2077, a customizable character whose background, appearance, and life path are defined by the player. Born and raised in Night City, V navigates the dangerous world of corporate espionage, cybernetic enhancements, and street-level survival.

V's journey begins with a quest for the ultimate cyberware implant - the Relic, which contains the digital consciousness of the legendary rockerboy Johnny Silverhand. As V becomes entangled with Johnny's personality and memories, they must confront powerful corporations, criminal gangs, and their own mortality in a world where technology and humanity blur.

With three distinct lifepaths to choose from - Street Kid, Corpo, or Nomad - V's story can unfold in countless ways. Each choice shapes their reputation, relationships, and ultimately, their destiny in Night City's neon-soaked underworld. Whether you're a netrunner hacking through corporate firewalls or a solo brawling through gang territories, V's journey is one of self-discovery, survival, and the search for meaning in a world that has lost its way.
